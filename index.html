Good questions

Before you try it.

Is Beyond Words another speech-to-text app?

It starts with speech recognition, but the product experience is about producing usable text while preserving what you meant to say, not silently rewriting it.

Will it rewrite or summarise what I say?

No, not by default. Faithful cleanup is the default. Rewriting, summarising, translation or structured formatting should happen only when you explicitly request that transformation.

What happens when it is unsure what I said?

Uncertain wording should be preserved or clearly flagged rather than confidently guessed. The product should not invent names, technical terms, commitments or missing context.

Which apps and operating systems are supported?

The supported platform and application compatibility list will be published with the release. We will not claim integrations that have not been validated.

How is my voice data handled?

The exact processing, retention, model-use and deletion details will be published before public sign-up opens. Please do not share sensitive information through an early-access request until those details are available.

When will early access and pricing be available?

Availability and pricing have not been announced. This page will be updated when the first release and onboarding details are confirmed.
