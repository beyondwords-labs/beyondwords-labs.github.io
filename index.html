The intelligence is in knowing what not to change

Your words.
Not someone else’s version.

We don't rewrite your thoughts to sound more polished. We correct clear recognition mistakes, remove non-semantic speech noise and apply formatting when you explicitly ask for it.

● THREE EXAMPLES OF RESTRAINT
✓
Clear transcription correctionCorrect what the recogniser got wrong

“Let’s do it tm no on—let’s do it tonight.”

“Let’s do it tonight.”

The recogniser produced a garbled fragment. The speaker immediately corrected themselves; the final intended wording is retained, not expanded into a new sentence.
≈
Conservative speech cleanupRemove noise, preserve meaningful language

“I think, um, we should probably check this again.”

“I think we should probably check this again.”

Removes the non-semantic filler “um”. Keeps “I think” and “probably” because they express the speaker’s level of certainty.
≡
Explicit formatting instructionStructure only when the speaker asks

“First, finish the deck. Second, call Priya. Third, review on Thursday.”

1. First, finish the deck.
2. Second, call Priya.
3. Third, review on Thursday.

Uses the speaker’s explicit sequence. No tasks, owners or deadlines are invented.

Faithful transcription is the default. Clear self-corrections are applied; uncertain fragments are preserved or flagged rather than guessed. Rewriting, summarising, translating or changing tone is never silently applied.
